#Robot descriptions

A model whose assembly declares joints (docs/ir.md "Joints") exports as a robot description, by suffix: cad model export ID robot.urdf, robot.xml (or .mjcf) for MuJoCo, robot.srdf for MoveIt. The crate is crates/cad-robot; the server's export route and the CLI call it.

#What a description holds

  • Links. One per joint (the parts the joint places: those in its moves whose last joint in

    chain order it is), and the root: the parts no joint moves. The root's link is named base, hung from an empty base_link on a fixed base_link_joint (KDL takes no inertia on a root); with no unmoved part the root is base_link itself. A joint's child link is named after its child's last label, made unique with _2.

  • Frames. Every link frame has the document's axes. A revolute or prismatic joint's child link

    frame sits on its axis origin as built; a fixed joint's child shares its parent's. So every <origin rpy> is 0 0 0, a joint's <origin xyz> is the difference of the two frames, and its <axis> is the declared direction, unit length.

  • Zero. The as-built pose is every joint's zero. Limits are written as offsets from it,

    [min − default, max − default], in radians or metres. A revolute without limits is continuous; a prismatic without limits is written ± the model's extent, with a warning.

  • Drive limits. The IR declares none, so effort and velocity are written 0, with a

    warning (cad urdf validate warns zero_effort/zero_velocity).

  • Meshes. One binary STL per part, in metres and in its link's frame, at the viewer's mesh

    tolerance, named <model>_<part path>.stl: in meshes/ beside a URDF (or package://NAME/meshes/ with --package NAME), in assets/ beside an MJCF (its meshdir). Each part is its link's visual and its collision (MuJoCo collides a mesh as its convex hull).

  • Inertials. Exact: each part's mass is its kernel volume × its material's density; its centre

    of mass is the kernel's; its tensor comes from the part's closed mesh at a quarter of the viewer's tolerance, scaled to that mass, and the link's is the sum about the link's centre of mass (parallel axes), in kg and kg·m². A part with no material weighs nothing, and the export says so; a moving link without mass is warned of (a simulator refuses it).

  • Poses. Named poses become MJCF <key>s (qpos in body order, radians/metres from as built)

    and SRDF <group_state>s. URDF has none.

#Formats

  • URDF: <material>s by colour, then the root link, then each joint followed by its child,

    depth first. Numbers have up to 9 significant digits. The same model writes the same bytes.

  • MJCF: <compiler angle="radian" meshdir="assets" autolimits="true"/>, bodies nested by the

    tree with a hinge/slide joint at each body's origin, <inertial fullinertia>, one mesh geom per part, keyframes. A scene.xml (the robot included, a floor, a light) is written once beside it and never overwritten: it is yours to edit.

  • SRDF: one group named after the robot, a <chain> from base_link to the tip where the

    movable joints make one chain, else the joints listed; a <group_state> per pose (every joint of the group); <disable_collisions> for each joint's two links (Adjacent) and for each pair of links with geometry that never touch over the sampled poses (Never): as built, every named pose, each joint alone at 0, ¼, ½, ¾ and all of its range, and 24 fixed pseudo-random spreads.

#Checks

cad urdf validate, cad srdf validate and cad mujoco check print one line per finding, severity: code at location: message Hint: …, then OK file: summary or FAILED file: …. Exit 0 passes, 1 fails (an error, or any warning with --strict), 2 refused. --json prints the report as one object {file, ok, findings: [{severity, code, at, message, hint}], summary}.

cad mujoco check also runs MuJoCo's own compile when python3 -c "import mujoco" works (mujoco_compiled / mujoco_compile), else says it was skipped (mujoco_skipped).

A clean check is a guardrail, not proof: it cannot tell that an axis or a sign is the robot's. Move each joint through its limits (cad model snapshot ID out.png --pose joint=value).

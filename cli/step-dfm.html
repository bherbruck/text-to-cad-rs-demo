Can it be made? The manufacturing check of the file's parts (see `cad model dfm`)

Usage: cad step dfm [OPTIONS] <FILE>

Arguments:
  <FILE>  

Options:
      --process <PROCESS>            printing, sheet, cnc or molding
      --material <MATERIAL>          The material (aluminum, steel, abs, petg, ...)
      --technology <TECHNOLOGY>      printing: fdm, sla, sls, mjf or pbf
      --build-volume <BUILD_VOLUME>  printing: the printer's build volume, XxYxZ mm
      --angle-limit <ANGLE_LIMIT>    printing: the printer's self-supporting angle from horizontal, degrees
      --layer-height <LAYER_HEIGHT>  printing: layer height, mm
      --nozzle <NOZZLE>              printing (FDM): nozzle diameter, mm
      --thickness <THICKNESS>        sheet: the stock's thickness, mm
      --bend-radius <BEND_RADIUS>    sheet: the shop's inside bend radius, mm
      --axes <AXES>                  cnc: 3, 4 or 5
      --tools <TOOLS>                cnc: end mill diameters, mm: 3,6,10
      --pull <PULL>                  molding: the pull direction: z, x, -y or x,y,z
      --wall-limit <WALL_LIMIT>      molding: faces steeper than this from the parting plane are walls, degrees
      --strict                       A warning fails the check too
      --json                         Print the result as one line of JSON
  -h, --help                         Print help

cad model render

A path-traced picture (draft by default; --preset final for the finished look), or a turntable as a GIF or a PNG sequence

A path-traced picture (draft by default; --preset final for the finished look), or a turntable as a GIF or a PNG sequence

Usage: cad model render [OPTIONS] <ID> <OUT>

Arguments:
  <ID>   
  <OUT>  OUT.png, OUT.gif (with --turntable), or a folder / out_%03d.png for a PNG sequence

Options:
      --preset <PRESET>            draft (800×600, 16 samples) or final (1600×1200, 256 samples, 6 bounces)
      --camera <CAMERA>            iso, front, back, left, right, top, bottom, AZ:EL in degrees, or x,y,z toward the camera
      --size <SIZE>                WIDTHxHEIGHT
      --samples <SAMPLES>          Paths per pixel
      --bounces <BOUNCES>          Bounces per path
      --lens <LENS>                Focal length, mm (50)
      --fstop <FSTOP>              Depth of field: the f-number
      --environment <ENVIRONMENT>  studio, sky, white or dark
      --background <BACKGROUND>    environment, transparent, white, black or #rrggbb
      --ground <GROUND>            shadow, plane or none
      --pose <POSE>                A pose: a name, joint=value,... or JSON
      --animation <ANIMATION>      An animation's name (with --time, or played over a --turntable with --animate)
      --time <TIME>                Seconds into --animation
      --part <PART>                Only this part (its path)
      --seed <SEED>                Seed: the same options render the same image
      --turntable                  Turn the camera about the model: a GIF (OUT.gif) or a PNG sequence
      --frames <FRAMES>            Turntable frames (24)
      --fps <FPS>                  Turntable frames per second of a GIF (12)
      --degrees <DEGREES>          Turntable degrees (360)
      --animate                    Play --animation over the turntable
      --json <OPTIONS>             Every render option as JSON (docs/api.md "Render"); flags override it
  -h, --help                       Print help

#3D printing

Read this file before modelling or checking a part that is 3D printed: FDM, SLA, SLS, MJF or metal powder bed (PBF). What every process shares is the review reference.

#Design it

The numbers are FDM's unless another technology is named: per figure, the first of Protolabs Network's (Hubs), Protolabs' and Xometry's design guides that gives one; cad's own where none does.

  • Model it as it prints. Z up, standing on its lowest face: the check measures the part as it

    stands. For another orientation, turn the part in its model and check again.

  • Overhangs. A down-facing slope at least 45 degrees from horizontal prints without support (FDM,

    PBF; SLA 19). On FDM a flat ceiling bridges at most 5 mm, and a horizontal round hole prints unsupported up to 10 mm (cad's own; wider, a teardrop, or turn the part). SLS and MJF need no support.

  • Walls. FDM: 0.8 mm, two beads of a 0.4 mm nozzle (of the --nozzle given). Elsewhere, a wall

    joined on two sides or more and a free one: SLA 0.5 and 1.0, SLS 0.8 and 1.0, MJF 0.7 and 1.0, PBF 0.4 and 0.8. On FDM a skin lying in the layer plane needs two layers (one at least).

  • Holes and pins. Holes 1 mm or wider (SLS and PBF 1.5, SLA 0.8), vertical where they can be.

    Pins 1 mm (SLS 0.8, PBF 0.6, SLA and MJF 0.5).

  • No knife edges. A convex edge with under 45 degrees of material thins to nothing: blunt it or

    open it to 45 degrees.

  • Hollow parts. SLA, SLS, MJF and PBF need an escape hole for each sealed cavity; FDM prints one

    as infill.

  • Fits. Parts printed together, in place, fuse under a clearance of 0.76 mm (Xometry; SLA 0.5,

    SLS 0.3, MJF 0.9, PBF 0.64).

#Check it

cad model dfm "$ID" --process printing --technology sla --build-volume 145x145x185
cad model dfm "$ID" --process printing --nozzle 0.6 --layer-height 0.28
  • --technology fdm|sla|sls|mjf|pbf (default fdm): every limit is the technology's, and a rule

    it has no figure for is not evaluated.

  • --material: a metal with a plastic technology, or a plastic with pbf, is refused.
  • --build-volume XxYxZ: the person's printer, an error to exceed. With none, a typical machine of

    the technology's is assumed (build_volume_assumed), exceeding it a warning, and the largest machine the guide lists an error. The fit is of the part's sorted sizes against the machine's.

  • --nozzle (FDM): the wall minimum is two beads of it. --layer-height: an FDM skin's layers.
  • --angle-limit: the printer's own self-supporting angle in place of the technology's.

#Read it

The families of measurements: wall_thickness (each supported by its joins: true on two sides or more, false on one, null where none was read, judged as supported and warned of between the two minima), overhangs (slope, ceiling, bridge; angle_deg from horizontal), bridges (length_mm, the shorter span), holes (orientation), pins, gaps (slots within a part), clearances (between parts), knife_edges, trapped_volumes (sealed cavities), build_volume, sections (PBF).

#Review the part

Check by hand what the check does not measure: embossed and engraved detail (said as families_not_measured), support removal and finish, stair-stepping, warping of long flat FDM parts, a cavity open to the outside through a narrow vent (only sealed ones are found), lattices and tolerances.

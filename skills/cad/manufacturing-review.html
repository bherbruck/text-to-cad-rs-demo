#Manufacturing checks: the result, the review, a redesign

Read this file for a manufacturing check's result in full, a shop's own limits, a review or a redesign. It is what every process shares: SKILL.md's Can it be made? says when a check runs, and each process's reference what is particular to it.

#The check

cad model dfm "$ID" --process P [options] measures the document as the last run left it, on the server's kernel: every part, as it stands in the model, each finding at a face or edge ref (bracket/face 3, plate/edge 16) that cad model inspect "$ID" '<ref>' resolves. Check a part by its own model's id where an assembly holds several; an assembly's check judges every part where it stands (a print's overhangs and a molding's draft turn with the part).

What it measures, on any kernel: walls and gaps by rays across the material, holes and pins from cylindrical faces, the material's angle at every edge (knife edges, inside corners), sealed cavities, what a straight tool or a mold half reaches. A family it does not measure is said (families_not_measured), never passed.

#Read the result

cad model dfm "$ID" --process cnc --tools 8,10, on a plate whose pocket corners are 3 mm:

error: cnc.internal_corner_radius, 4 place(s). Hint: radius the corner to at least 4 mm, the smallest tool's radius, or add a smaller tool. Source: Protolabs Network (Hubs), How to design parts for CNC machining.
  plate/face 9: internal corner radius 3 mm is under the 4 mm minimum (10 mm tall)
  ...
info: material_declared at bodies: material aluminum, read from the parts' declared material: give --material to judge another
FAILED 6c29e879-…: 4 error(s), 1 warning(s); cnc 3-axis milling: 1 part(s), 42000 mm3, 2 setup(s) (+Z -Z), 1 pocket(s); failed: cnc.internal_corner_radius x4

A failing rule is said once, with its hint and its source, then a line for each feature at its ref; any other finding is one line. The last line is the check's state, and so is the exit status:

Last lineExitstatusIt says
OK <id>: [N warning(s); ]<what was measured>0okevery part was measured and no finding is an error. Not an approval: warnings (recommendations) remain
FAILED <id>: E error(s)[, W warning(s)]; <what>; failed: <code> xN, ...1faileda finding is an error: the part cannot be made by this process as drawn. With --strict, a warning fails too
NOT CHECKED <id>: <code> ...3not_checkedthe check did not judge what it was asked to: never a pass

Exit 2 is the command refused (an unknown option, a value of the wrong kind, a metal for molding): its message says the fix, which is in the command, not the part.

--json prints the result as one line: status, ok, reason, summary, findings and measurements. Key on status or the exit, never on a finding's severity.

  • Not checked. not_solid (a part that is no closed solid), document_empty,

    measurement_failed (a family the kernel could not measure), rule_partly_judged (items a rule had no figure for: its refs) and rules_none_evaluated (no rule has a limit for this context, as a technology no guide covers). A part that fails and was not wholly checked is FAILED, its line ending ; not checked: <code> xN.

  • Order. What puts every figure in doubt comes first: what was not checked, not_sound (the

    kernel's validity check fails) and units_suspect (a part under 1 mm or over 10 m across). Then errors, warnings and facts.

  • A rule's finding is data. In --json its code is the rule's id (rule), with value,

    limit, unit, op (min: at least; max: at most), source and refs: every ref of the feature, the most specific first (a corner's edge, then its faces). Edit from those, never from the message. Several failures of one rule at one feature are one finding (and 2 more at this ref); a recommendation on a figure that already fails a stricter rule there is left to measurements.rules.

  • Whose limit. A limit no published guide states is cad's own floor, and its source says so

    (cad, its own figure: ...): report it as that, never as a guide's.

  • Facts. A code that is no rule's is a fact or an assumption: material_declared (read from

    the part), an option assumed (cnc.tools_assumed, resin_unknown, build_volume_assumed, nozzle_assumed, sheet.thickness_measured: report it), several_solids (one part of several solids).

  • The figures. measurements.<family>.items hold every feature measured (with location,

    xyz and its figures), not only the failing ones. measurements.rules.outcomes has one entry for every rule:

    statusReport it as
    passeda pass; in rules.none_found it had nothing of its kind to judge: say none was found
    faileda failure: an error (not made as drawn) or a warning (a recommendation)
    partiala pass of the features it judged alone: rule_partly_judged names the rest
    not_evaluatedunverified, never a pass: no limit here, or the family was not measured
    not_applicablenothing: the rule is for another context

#A shop's own limits

Each process has one table of rules: general practice from published design guides (Protolabs Network (Hubs), Protolabs, Xometry), and cad's own floors where no guide states a limit. None is a shop's. What the person knows of their shop goes in as the process's options (the shop's tools, the printer's build volume and nozzle, the stock's thickness), and a shop's other limits are compared with measurements.<family>.

A specification the person supplies overrides every rule: compare its numbers with the measured figures and cite it.

#The review

The check is evidence, not a certification. A review says:

  • the scope: the model and its id, the parts, the process, the material, and what the check was

    told of the shop or assumed;

  • the findings: each feature at its ref with the measured value, the rule with its limit and

    source, and a specific change (the hint, with this part's numbers). Keep errors (constraints) and warnings (recommendations) apart;

  • the coverage: the rules not evaluated, the families not measured, and the process

    reference's Review the part list.

#Check a redesign

Edit the model's source, run it, and run the same check with the same options. The finding's code should be gone from its place; a new one elsewhere is the redesign's. Dropping or shrinking a feature the person specified to pass a check is a different design: say so.

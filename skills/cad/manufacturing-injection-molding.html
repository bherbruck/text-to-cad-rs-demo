#Injection molding

Read this file before modelling or checking a molded plastic part. What every process shares is the review reference.

#Design it

Figures from Protolabs' and Protolabs Network's (Hubs) molding guides; cad's own floors where they give none.

  • Draft. Every wall along the pull tapers: 0.5 degrees at least, 2 where the design allows (more

    on tall or textured walls). A wall that runs more than 10 mm along the pull in one mold half with no draft fails (0.01 degree for every millimetre past that, 0.25 at most). Taper a wall with a cone or extrude(h, { taper }).

  • Undercuts. Every surface faces one mold half and sees out along the pull. A side hole or slot

    needs a side action (tooling cost, a warning); a feature nothing releases fails.

  • Walls: uniform, within the resin's range (ABS 1.1 to 3.6 mm; Protolabs' table per resin).

    Under half the resin's minimum does not fill (an error); a section more than 1.7 times the nominal wall sinks.

#Check it

cad model dfm "$ID" --process molding --material abs
cad model dfm "$ID" --process molding --pull x
  • --pull (default z): the mold's opening direction, x, y, z, -z or three numbers.
  • --material: the resin (abs, pc, pp, nylon, pom, ...); a metal is refused. With none, walls are

    judged against every resin's range (resin_unknown).

  • --wall-limit (default 45): faces steeper than this from the parting plane are walls.

#Read it

The families: draft (each wall's least draft_deg, half_depth_mm), undercuts (release: side_action with its release_dir, or none; trapped_area_mm2), thickness (per wall, thickness_mm and ratio_to_nominal), pull. molding.nominal_wall says the nominal wall.

#Review the part

Check by hand: ribs and bosses (their own ratios are not read), gate and parting line placement, clamp tonnage, shrinkage and texture.

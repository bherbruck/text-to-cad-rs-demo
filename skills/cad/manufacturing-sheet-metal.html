#Sheet metal

Read this file before modelling or checking a part cut and bent from sheet. What every process shares is the review reference.

#Design it

Figures from Protolabs Network's (Hubs) and Protolabs' sheet metal guides; cad's own where they give none.

  • One thickness throughout. No boss, rib, pocket or step: every face of the sheet has its

    opposite face a thickness across; a bend is an inside radius r and an outside radius r + t about one axis. Model it as a profile extruded to the part's width, with arcs at the bends.

  • Thickness: 0.5 to 6 mm to bend, to 10 mm to laser cut; over 25.4 mm is plate, not sheet.
  • Bends: inside radius at least the thickness; over 10 thicknesses is bump-formed or rolled.
  • Holes: at least the thickness across, the thickness from the edge, two thicknesses from each

    other and from a bend's tangent line. Flanges at least 2 t + r high.

#Check it

cad model dfm "$ID" --process sheet --material steel --thickness 2
  • --thickness: the stock's gauge; with none, the sheet's thickness is measured

    (sheet.thickness_measured), and every rule that scales with it uses it.

  • --material: a metal (a plastic is refused).

#Read it

The families: parts (thickness_mm, is_sheet, bent), not_sheet (faces that depart from one sheet: thicker or thinner, local_thickness_mm), bends (inside_radius_mm, radius_to_thickness, angle_deg; a sharp inside edge between sheet faces is a bend of radius 0), holes (size_mm, edge_distance_mm), hole_spacing, hole_to_bend. A part under half of whose surface is a constant thickness across, or whose sheet is not mostly flat (a round wire, a spring, a rolled tube), is sheet.not_sheet (an error).

#Review the part

Not measured (said as families_not_measured): flange heights and bend reliefs. Check by hand, with the flat pattern from the shop: reliefs at each bend's ends, notches and tabs, the K-factor, and the grain direction.
